How it works

Three steps and you are in.

Everything happens on our own accounts, so there is nothing to fill in twice and no separate form to lose.

01

Create your AREC account

Every AREC service uses the same sign-in. Make one account on our auth server and use it everywhere — the TMS, events, and every tool we run. It takes about a minute, and you will want multi-factor authentication set up anyway.

Create an account Already have one? Sign in
02

Sign in to the TMS

The Team Management System is where your team lives. Sign in with your AREC account (or with HackClub if that is what you use) and you land in your dashboard.

Open the TMS Browse events
03

Create your team — that is the registration

Set up your team, add your teammates and mentors, and you are pre-registered for the 2027 season. There is no second form to submit. When fees are finalised and registration opens on October 26, your team is already in the system and any discount applies automatically.

Go to the TMS
One account, everything The TMS, our auth server, and every other AREC service share a single sign-in. If you already have an account from volunteering or from running an event, use it — you do not need a second one.
Before you start

Have this to hand.

You can create a team with very little, but having these ready saves a round of emails later.

  • Team nameWhatever you want to be called
  • School, org, or groupOr "independent" if you are unaffiliated
  • Captain contactName and email
  • Team numberOptional — assigned if you skip it
  • How many studentsRoughly, for kit planning
  • Adult mentorsWho is signing off for your team
What you get

Register once, and it counts.

Because registration happens on the TMS, everything we do for teams flows from the same record — no forms to resubmit, no details that fall out of sync.

Discounts applied automatically

Whatever fee structure and discount we settle on gets applied to your team without you doing anything. There is no code to remember.

Event registration, sorted

Sign up for the events you want to attend from the same account. Events open for registration on October 30.

Workshops and kits

New teams get hands-on build workshops and mentorship, and we can work out which teams need a subsidised kit or spare parts.

Good to know

Before you hit the button.

When does it open?

Team registration opens on October 26, 2026. You can create your account and your team ahead of time, but the season starts on the 26th.

What does it cost?

Fees are still to be announced and should be confirmed within a week of registration opening. Nothing is charged at sign-up.

Do we have to be a school?

No. Schools, community organizations, and fully independent student teams are all eligible. If your school will not back you, we still want you in the league.

Do I need a robot already?

No. Plenty of teams register first and build later — that is what the free workshops and mentorship are for.

Can I run an event too?

Yes. Event applications open on October 24, and you can apply to run a community or official event at the same time as registering a team.

What if I am stuck?

Email join@arecrobotics.com and a human will walk you through it.
