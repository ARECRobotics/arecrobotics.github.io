Sign in to the TMS

Pre-register your team.

There is no separate form. Creating your team on the TMS is the pre-registration, and any discount we announce is applied to your account automatically.

How to register

Three steps, all on our own accounts.

  1. 01

    Create your AREC account

    Every AREC service uses the same sign-in. Make one account on the auth server and use it for the TMS, events, and every tool we run. It takes about a minute.

    Create an account Already have one
  2. 02

    Sign in to the TMS

    Sign in with your AREC account, or with HackClub if that is what you use. You land in your dashboard.

    Open the TMS Browse events
  3. 03

    Create your team

    Set up your team, add your teammates and mentors, and you are pre-registered. There is no second form. When fees are finalised and registration opens on October 26, your team is already in the system.

    Go to the TMS

What to have ready

You can register with very little. This saves a round of emails later.

team name Whatever you want to be called.
school or group Your school, organization, or community group. Write "independent" if you are unaffiliated.
captain contact The name and email of whoever is running the team.
team number Optional. We assign one if you skip it.
students Roughly how many, so we can plan kits.
adult mentors Who signs off for the team.

What registering gets you

Everything we do for teams comes from the same record.

discounts Applied automatically. Whatever fee structure and discount we settle on reaches your team without you doing anything.
events Sign up for the events you want from the same account. Events open for registration on October 30.
workshops and kits Build workshops and mentorship for new teams. We also work out which teams need a subsidised kit or spare parts.

Before you start

Straight answers to the questions we get most.

  • When does it open Team registration opens on October 26, 2026. You can create your account and your team ahead of time.
  • What does it cost Fees are still to be announced, and should be confirmed within a week of registration opening. Nothing is charged at sign-up.
  • Do we have to be a school No. Schools, community organizations, and independent student teams are all eligible. If your school will not back you, we still want you in the league.
  • Do I need a robot already No. Plenty of teams register first and build later. That is what the free workshops and mentorship are for.
  • Can I run an event too Yes. Event applications open on October 24, so you can apply to run a community or official event alongside registering a team.
  • What if I am stuck Email join@arecrobotics.com and a person will walk you through it.
