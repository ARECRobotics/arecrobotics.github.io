03 Register

Registration open

Ten minutes.
One account.
That is the signup.

No separate form, no fee at signup and no robot required. You need a team name and a rough idea of who is on the team.

01 How to register

Three steps, all in one place.

Your account and your team live in the same system, so you only fill things in once.

01

Make an AREC account

One account works across the team dashboard, event signups and workshops. It takes about a minute.

Create account Sign in
02

Open the team dashboard

Sign in with your AREC account, or with Hack Club if that is what you use. You land on your dashboard.

Open dashboard Browse events
03

Create your team

Add a team name, your teammates and an adult mentor. When fees are announced, your team is already in the system.

Go to dashboard

Nothing is charged when you register.

Any discount we announce is applied to your team automatically. You do not have to claim it.

Fees · announced soon

02 Before you start

What to have ready.

You do not need a robot or a decision from your school. Bring what you have and fill in the rest later.

Team name
Whatever you want to be called.
School or group
Your school, club or community group.Write independent if you are not with one.
Captain contact
A name and email for whoever runs the team.
Team number
Optional. We assign one if you skip it.
Students
A rough number, so we can plan workshops.
Adult mentor
Who signs off for the team.

If you only have a team name right now, that is enough to start. Adding people later is easy, and it gets you on the workshop list sooner.

Team registration opened on October 26, 2026. You can make your account and your team now.

03 What you get

Set up once, use it all season.

Your team details feed event signups, workshops and the season schedule. You will not be asked for them twice.

  • Discounts, applied for you Whatever fee structure we settle on, any discount is applied to your team automatically.
  • Event signups Pick the events you want from the same dashboard. Event registration opens October 30.
  • Workshops and mentors Build sessions for new teams, plus help finding the hardware you need.
  • A place in the season A team number and a spot on the 2027 schedule.

04 Questions

The ones we get most.

If yours is not here, email us. A real person will answer.

When did registration open?

Team registration opened on October 26, 2026. You can make your account and team now.

What does it cost?

Fees are still being decided and will be announced within a week of registration opening. Nothing is charged at signup.

Do we need to be a school?

No. Schools, clubs and independent teams can all join. If your school will not back you, come anyway.

Do we need a robot first?

No. Lots of teams sign up before they have built anything. That is what the workshops and mentors are for.

Can we host an event too?

Yes. Event applications open October 24, so you can apply to host a community or official event.

What if we get stuck?

Email join@arecrobotics.com and we will walk you through it.
