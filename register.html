How To Register

Three steps, all on our own accounts. It takes about ten minutes.

Nothing is charged when you sign up, and any discount we announce is applied to your team without you doing anything about it.

Create your account

Every AREC service uses the same sign in. Make one account on the auth server and use it for the team management system, for events, and for every tool we run.

Create an account Already have one

Sign in to the TMS

Sign in with your AREC account, or with HackClub if that is what you use. You land in your dashboard.

Open the TMS Browse events

Create your team

Set up your team, add your teammates and mentors, and you are pre-registered. There is no second form. When fees are finalised and registration opens on October 26, your team is already in the system.

Go to the TMS

What To Have Ready

You can register with very little. This saves a round of emails later.

None of this needs a robot, and none of it needs a decision from your school. Bring what you have.

Team name Whatever you want to be called.
School or group Your school, organisation, or community group. Write independent if you are not affiliated with one.
Captain contact The name and email of whoever is running the team.
Team number Optional. We assign one if you skip it.
Students Roughly how many, so we can plan workshops.
Adult mentors Who signs off for the team.

What Registering Gets You

Everything we do for teams comes from the same record.

Discounts Applied automatically. Whatever fee structure and discount we settle on reaches your team without you doing anything.
Events Sign up for the events you want from the same account. Events open for registration on October 30.
Workshops Build workshops and mentorship for new teams, and help finding the hardware you need.
A place in the season Your team is on the list for the 2027 season, with a team number and a schedule.

The sooner you register, the sooner we can put you on a workshop and sort out the hardware side of things.

Before You Start

Straight answers to the questions we get most.

When does it open

Team registration opens on October 26, 2026. You can create your account and your team ahead of time.

What does it cost

Fees are still to be announced, and should be confirmed within a week of registration opening. Nothing is charged at sign up.

Do we have to be a school

No. Schools, community organisations, and independent student teams are all eligible. If your school will not back you, we still want you in the league.

Do I need a robot already

No. Plenty of teams register first and build later. That is what the free workshops and the mentorship are for.

Can I run an event too

Yes. Event applications open on October 24, so you can apply to run a community or official event alongside registering a team.

What if I am stuck

Email join@arecrobotics.com and a person will walk you through it.
