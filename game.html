Season timeline

How the season opens up.

All of these fall in 2026, feeding straight into the 2027 competition season. Dates are fixed unless we say otherwise.

Season name Coming soon. We are still working on the name — but the season is coming either way.
Oct 24
2026

Event creation opens

Anyone can apply to run an event — both community events and official ones. If you have a venue, a field, and the drive to run a competition, this is your entry point.

Oct 26
2026

Team registration opens

Pre-registration for the 2027 season goes live. Creating your team on the TMS counts as pre-registration — any registration discount is applied automatically. Fees are still to be announced and should be confirmed within the week.

Oct 30
2026

Event registration opens

Sign up for the events you want to attend. Event applications and team sign-ups both happen inside the TMS, so everything stays in one place.

Nov 1
2026

Game released publicly

After we settle the game mechanics, the game goes live on the website. Rules, field layout, and everything you need to start designing around it land on this date.

Nov 9
2026

Game piece store opens

The online store opens for game piece orders, for teams that would rather not print their own. Downloading the STL and printing it yourself is still an option.

Nov 23
2026

Trophies and plaques

The online store opens for award trophies and plaques. Details are still to be announced.

Still to be announced

What we do not know yet.

We would rather tell you nothing than tell you something wrong. Here is the short list of things we have not locked down yet.

Season name

The 2027 season needs a name. It is being worked on now — hold onto your team name puns.

Registration fees

Fees for the 2027 season are still to be announced, and should be confirmed within a week of team registration opening on October 26.

Trophies and plaques

The trophy and plaque store opens on November 23, but what is available and what it costs is still to be announced.

Registration opens October 26.

Pre-register your team on the TMS. It takes a couple of minutes, and any discount we end up announcing is applied to your account automatically.
