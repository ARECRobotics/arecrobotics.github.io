Season timeline

All of these fall in 2026, feeding the 2027 competition season.

  1. Oct 24

    Event creation opens

    Anyone can apply to run an event, community or official. You need a venue, a field, and the drive to run a competition.

  2. Oct 26

    Team registration opens

    Pre-registration for the 2027 season goes live. Creating your team on the TMS counts as pre-registration, and any discount applies automatically. Fees are still to be announced.

  3. Oct 30

    Event registration opens

    Sign up for the events you want to attend. Event applications and team signups both happen inside the TMS.

  4. Nov 1

    Game released publicly

    Once we settle the game mechanics, the game goes live on the website. Rules, field layout, and everything you need to design around it land on this date.

  5. Nov 9

    Game piece store opens

    The store opens for game piece orders, for teams that would rather not print their own. The STL stays free either way.

  6. Nov 23

    Trophies and plaques

    The store opens for award trophies and plaques. Details are still to be announced.
