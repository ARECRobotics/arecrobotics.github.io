About

AREC exists to lower the cost of entry into robotics — the financial, structural, and social barriers that keep kids out of STEM before they even get a chance to try it.

We run a competitive robotics league built around low-cost robots. Cutting hardware and registration costs means under-resourced schools, community organizations, and independent student teams can compete alongside anyone else.

Beyond the robots themselves, we offer mentorship, workshops, and a network that helps new teams get off the ground and keep running year after year.

How it works

Who runs it

Current high school students manage day-to-day operations, tournament logistics, and peer mentorship. There's no separate adult-run organization behind the curtain — it's students, top to bottom.

What it teaches

Running a league gives organizers real project management, engineering, and leadership experience — skills that carry over long after they've graduated.

Why it's set up this way

A student-led structure keeps the organization close to the people it's actually for, and builds a habit of community service that outlasts any one season.

The league

A shared, low-cost robot

Every team builds on the same affordable hardware platform, so the contest comes down to engineering and strategy, not who spent more.

Mentorship and workshops

New teams get hands-on workshops and ongoing mentorship to help them build a robot, find funding, and show up ready to compete.

Open to any team

Under-resourced schools, community groups, and independent student teams are all eligible to register and compete.
