Who We Are

A student run robotics league for teams that cannot buy their way in.

AREC puts on a season of events where every team builds its own robot, drives it over WiFi, and plays on the same field. The rules are short so the engineering decides the match, not the budget.

Join

Create a team on the team management system. That is the registration. There is no second form and nothing is charged on the day.

Build

Put a robot together. Chassis, motors, motor driver and brain are your call. The only fixed part is the brain, and it has to speak WiFi.

Drive

Run the AREC driver app on a laptop or a tablet and drive over WiFi. Join the venue network or run your own access point, whichever is easier.

Compete

Sign up for the events you want, turn up, and play. Scoring, referees and a printed game piece are all handled by us.

What A Team Gets

Everything you need to build a robot, learn how to drive it, and put it on a field.

Support that is included, not sold on the side. Workshops, mentorship, the rule set and the money to make it all work.

Build it yourself

Nothing about the robot is fixed. Any chassis, any motors, any motor driver. Build it from parts you already own or buy the build bundle from the store and fit your own brain.

WiFi driving

The brain connects to the driver app over WiFi and nothing else. Either join the venue network or let the robot run its own access point and connect straight to it.

Free workshops

Hands on sessions where we help you get a robot built and working. No experience needed, and no charge for teams in the league.

One rule set

The same rules for everybody. Size, weight, voltage and the WiFi driving rule, published before the season starts so you can build to it.

Real events

Community and official events, refereed and scored, with a game piece you can print yourself or order from the store.

Money for robots

Sponsorships and donations pay for hardware, workshops and entry fees, so that no team is priced out of the league.
