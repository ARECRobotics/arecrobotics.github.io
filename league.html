01 The league

Season 2027

One season.
Same rules.
Different robots.

How the year works, what your team gets, and what is happening when. The short version: you build the robot, we bring the field.

02 How it works

You do not need a kit or a parts list.

You pick the chassis, the motors and the motor driver. You put a WiFi board in the middle. The only thing you cannot change is how the robot is driven.

  1. 01

    Register your team

    Create a team on the dashboard and you are in. That is the whole signup, and it costs nothing.

  2. 02

    Build your robot

    Any design that fits inside 18 inches, stays under 30 pounds and runs on 12 volts or less. Parts and tools are your call.

  3. 03

    Drive it over WiFi

    Use the AREC driver app on a laptop or tablet. No Bluetooth, no radio, no cable for driving. Everything goes over one WiFi link.

  4. 04

    Play the season

    Sign up for the events you want and turn up. Referees, scoring and the game pieces are all handled by us.

03 What teams get

Support that comes with the season.

Workshops, mentors, rules and money. None of it is an extra you have to chase.

  • Build workshops Hands-on sessions with parts on the table. Bring a laptop, leave with a moving robot.
  • A mentor for new teams Someone who has built a robot before, on the other end of an email.
  • The rulebook before the season Size, weight, power, driving and scoring, published with time to build.
  • Referees and scoring Every official event has a trained crew and a score that goes on the record.
  • Game pieces Print the model yourself for free, or order a printed piece from the store.
  • Money for parts Sponsorship pays for hardware and entry fees when a team needs the help.

05 The game piece

Free to print. Same piece for every team.

A better printer should not win a match. Download the model, print it at home, or order one when the store opens on November 9.

3D model
Download STL

Loading the game piece

Drag to turn it, scroll to zoom, click the piece.

This is a placeholder model. Add assets/game-element.stl and it loads on the next refresh.

The model is published as an STL, so you can print it on any machine that handles the size. We check every piece against the size limit before it goes on the field.

There is no advantage to printing it yourself or buying one. It is the same shape, the same size and the same material rules either way.

06 Sponsors

Every dollar lowers the cost of playing.

Sponsorship pays for hardware, workshop space and entry fees. It is how a team with no budget still gets a robot on the field.

Workshop sponsor

League sponsor
Keeps the season running, from fields and referees to game pieces.
