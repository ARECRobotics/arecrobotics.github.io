How The League Works

Every team builds its own robot. The rules are the same for all of them.

There is no reference robot and no parts list you have to follow. You pick a chassis, you pick motors, and you put a WiFi brain in it. The one thing you cannot change is how the robot is driven.

Register

Create a team on the team management system and you are in. That is the whole registration, and nothing is charged on the day.

Build

Any robot you like, as long as it fits inside 18 inches, stays under 30 pounds and runs on 12 volts or less.

Drive

The AREC driver app on a laptop or a tablet, talking to the brain over WiFi. No Bluetooth, no radio link, no serial cable for driving.

Compete

Sign up for the events you want, turn up, and play. Scoring, referees and the game piece are handled by us.

What A Team Gets

Support that is included, rather than sold on the side.

Workshops, mentorship, the rule set, and the money to make it all work. None of it is an extra you have to ask for.

Build it yourself

Nothing about the robot is fixed. Any chassis, any motors, any motor driver. Build it from parts you already own, or buy the build bundle from the store and fit your own brain.

WiFi driving

The brain connects to the driver app over WiFi and nothing else. Either join the venue network or let the robot run its own access point and connect straight to it.

Free workshops

Hands on sessions where we help you get a robot built and working. No experience needed, and no charge for teams in the league.

One rule set

The same rules for everybody. Size, weight, voltage and the WiFi driving rule, published before the season starts so you can build to it.

Real events

Community and official events, refereed and scored, with a game piece you can print yourself or order from the store.

Money for robots

Sponsorships and donations pay for hardware, workshops and entry fees, so that no team is priced out of the league.

The Game Piece

The game piece is free to print. We publish the model.

The official 2027 game piece is published as a model you can print at home. Order one from the store when it opens on November 9 if printing is not your thing.

Loading the game piece

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click the piece.

This is the placeholder model. Drop the real file at assets/game-element.stl and it loads on the next refresh.

Every team plays with the same piece, so nobody wins because they had a better print.

The model is published as an STL, so you can scale it, change it, or print it on any machine you like. We check the piece against the size limit before it goes on the field.
