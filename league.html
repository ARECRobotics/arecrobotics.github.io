How it works

Three things worth knowing before you turn up.

  1. 01

    Students run it

    Current high school students handle day-to-day operations, tournament logistics, and peer mentorship. There is no separate adult organization running things behind the scenes.

  2. 02

    Organizing is the education

    Running a league gives organizers real project management, engineering, and leadership experience. It stays with them well after they graduate.

  3. 03

    Close to the people it is for

    A student-led structure keeps the league near the teams it serves, and builds a habit of community service that outlasts a single season.

What a team gets

Support that is included, not sold separately.

hardware A shared, low-cost robot platform. Same parts for every team, so nobody wins on the budget.
mentorship Hands-on workshops and ongoing mentorship. We help you build a robot, find funding, and turn up ready to compete.
eligibility Open to under-resourced schools, community groups, and fully independent student teams.
cost Registration fees for the 2027 season are still to be announced. Discounts apply automatically to registered teams.
