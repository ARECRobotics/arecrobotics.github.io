How To Start

Three steps. No form, no fee, and no experience needed.

Almost every event volunteer was new on their first day. Event and logistics is the easiest place to start, and it is our biggest need.

Email us

Send a note to info@arecrobotics.com with the subject "I want to volunteer". Say where you are based and what you are interested in.

Create your account

Make an account on the auth server. It is the same sign in used across every AREC service, so one account covers the team management system and every tool we run. It takes about a minute.

Create an account Already have one

Wait for the meeting link

Once we have your account, we email a booking link to the address you used in step one. Pick a slot that works. In that meeting we work out what you will be doing and when you are needed.

Roles

Pick the one that sounds like you, or let us pick for you.

Most roles are trained on the day. If you are not sure where you fit, say so in your email and we will match you to whatever is useful at the time.

Event and logistics
Setup and teardown Build the field, set the barriers and game pieces, then strike it all down at the end of the day.
Field marshal Own the field between matches. Reset the game, clear debris, and keep the pace up.
Tech inspector Check every robot before it is allowed on the field. Size, weight, hardware, build rules.
Safety marshal Run the safety briefing and keep the pit and field areas safe.
Queue marshal Call and order the teams, run the ready queue, and start matches on time.
Check-in desk Welcome teams, hand out badges and schedules, and answer questions on arrival.
Match day
Referee Call the match. Know the rules inside out, make the call in the moment, then explain it to the teams.
Scorekeeper Track the score and the penalties for every match, and get it entered correctly first time.
Workshops and mentoring
Build mentor Work with one team through their season, from design to competition day.
Workshop facilitator Run hands on build sessions for new teams. You bring the expertise, we bring the parts and the room.
Outreach and media
Streamer and announcer Run the event stream and call the matches.
Photographer Capture the photos and footage people actually want to see.
School outreach Talk to schools, youth groups, and community organisations about putting a team together.
Fundraising Find the sponsorships and donations that pay for hardware and keep registration affordable.

What To Expect

Turn up, get trained, and help a match run.

Training On the day, before the doors open.
Minimum age Depends on the role and the venue.
Commitment Often just one event day.
Travel Reimbursed where we can.
Food Provided on event days.

AREC has no separate adult organisation behind it. Current high school students manage day to day operations, tournament logistics, and peer mentorship.

Working with us means working directly with the people running the league, and saying so when something is wrong.
