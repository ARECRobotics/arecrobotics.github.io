Volunteer

Run the league with us.

AREC is run by students, and that only works because people like you turn up. Most volunteers start at a competition — setting up fields, running matches, keeping scores — and end up running the whole league.

Email us See the roles

How to get started

Three steps.

There is no application form to fill out and nothing to pay. Just say hello, make an account, and we will find a time to talk.

01

Email us

Send a note to info@arecrobotics.com with the subject "I want to volunteer". Tell us roughly what you are interested in — event and logistics is the best place to start — and where you are based.

Email info@arecrobotics.com
02

Create your account

Make an account on our auth server at auth.albt.qzz.io. It is the same sign-in used across every AREC service, so one account covers the TMS, event tools, and everything else. It takes about a minute.

Create an account Already have one? Sign in
03

Wait for our meeting link

Once we have your account, we will email you a booking link to the address you used in step 1. Pick a slot that works, and that is where we work out exactly what you will be doing and when you are needed.

Meeting link arrives by email
Please use the same email We send the meeting link to the address you emailed us from in step 1, so keep an eye on that inbox as well as your new account. If the email never arrives, just reply to your original note and we will send it again.
Roles

Where you can help.

Event and logistics is our biggest need and the easiest way to start — most roles are trained on the day. But there is plenty of other work if you would rather be behind a laptop, in a classroom, or on the phone.

Event & logistics — the core of it

Competition

Setup & teardown crew

Build the field, lay the tape, set the game pieces and barriers, then strike it all down at the end of the day. The unglamorous job that makes everything else possible.

Competition

Field marshal

Own the field between matches — resetting the game, clearing debris, and keeping the pace up so the queue keeps moving.

Competition

Tech inspector

Check every robot before it is allowed on the field: size, weight, hardware, and any build rule it needs to meet.

Competition

Safety marshal

Keep the pit and field areas safe, run the safety briefing, and be the person anyone can flag a problem to.

Competition

Queue marshal

Call and order the teams, run the ready queue, and make sure matches start on time.

Competition

Check-in & registration desk

Welcome teams, hand out badges and schedules, and answer the questions people ask on arrival.

Match day officiating

Officiating

Referee

Call the match. Know the rules inside out and make the call in the moment, then explain it to the teams afterwards.

Officiating

Scorekeeper

Track the score and the penalties for every match, and get it entered correctly the first time.

Workshops & mentoring

Teaching

Build mentor

Work with one team through their season — helping them design, build, debug, and get to a competition day ready.

Teaching

Workshop facilitator

Run hands-on build sessions for new teams. You bring the expertise, we bring the parts and the room.

Outreach, media & fundraising

Comms

Streamer & media

Run the event stream, announce matches, and capture the footage and photos people actually want to see.

Outreach

School & community outreach

Talk to schools, community groups, and youth organizations about putting a team together and joining the league.

Fundraising

Sponsorships & fundraising

Help us find the sponsorships and donations that subsidize kits and keep registration affordable.

What to expect

No experience needed.

If you have never run a robotics competition before, that is completely fine — almost all of our event volunteers had never done it before their first one.

  • TrainingOn the day, before the doors open
  • Minimum ageDepends on the role and the venue
  • CommitmentOften just one event day
  • TravelReimbursed where we can
  • FoodProvided on event days
Who we are

Run by students.

There is no separate adult-run organization behind the curtain. Current high school students manage day-to-day operations, tournament logistics, and peer mentorship — so volunteering with us means working directly with the people running the league.

Not sure where you fit? Say so in your email. We will match you to whatever is most useful at the time, and you can always change roles later.
