04 Volunteer

No experience needed

Help run
the league.

Most people start at one event, setting up the field and keeping matches moving. You will be shown what to do before the doors open.

01 How to start

One email, one account, one short call.

Almost everyone was new on their first day. Field setup is the easiest place to start, and it is the help we need most.

01

Send us an email

Write to info@arecrobotics.com with the subject "I want to volunteer". Tell us where you are and what you would like to do.

02

Make an account

One AREC account covers everything we run, including the dashboard and event signups. It takes about a minute.

Create account Sign in
03

Pick a time to talk

We reply with a link to book a short call. We will work out what you will do and when we need you.

02 Roles

Pick what sounds like you. Or let us pick.

Most roles are taught on the day. If you are not sure where you fit, say so in your email and we will match you to what is useful.

Event and logistics
Setup and teardown Build the field, set out the barriers and game pieces, then pack it all away at the end of the day.
Field crew Keep the field moving between matches: reset the game, clear the floor and keep the pace up.
Robot check Measure, weigh and test every robot before it competes. Size, weight, power and the WiFi rule.
Safety lead Run the safety briefing and keep the pit and field areas safe.
Queue Call teams, line them up and start matches on time.
Check-in desk Welcome teams, hand out badges and schedules, and answer the first questions of the day.
Match day
Referee Call the match, know the rules and explain the call to both teams.
Scorekeeper Track the score and penalties, and get it entered right the first time.
Workshops and mentoring
Build mentor Work with one team through the season, from first sketch to competition day.
Workshop facilitator Run a hands-on build session. You bring the know-how; we bring the parts and the room.
Outreach and media
Streamer and announcer Run the event stream and call the matches for people watching at home.
Photographer Take the photos and footage that teams actually want to see.
School outreach Talk to schools, youth groups and clubs about starting a team.
Fundraising Find the sponsors and donations that pay for parts and keep registration low.

03 What to expect

Show up, get trained, help a match run.

No uniform, no shifts booked months ahead, and no separate adult organization behind the scenes.

Training
Before the doors open, on the day.
Age
Depends on the role and the venue. Ask us.
Time
Usually one event day.
Travel
Reimbursed when we can.
Food
Provided on event days.

AREC is run by high school students, not a separate adult organization. You will work directly with the people running the league.

That also means we want to hear it when something is wrong. If a process is confusing or a call was unfair, tell us. It is the fastest way to fix it for the next event.
