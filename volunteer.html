Volunteer

Volunteer with AREC.

The league runs on people turning up. Most volunteers start at a competition, setting up fields and running matches. Plenty end up running the whole league.

Email us See the roles

How to start

Three steps. No form, no fee.

  1. 01

    Email us

    Send a note to info@arecrobotics.com with the subject "I want to volunteer". Say where you are based and what you are interested in. Event and logistics is the easiest place to start.

  2. 02

    Create your account

    Make an account on the auth server at auth.albt.qzz.io. It is the same sign-in used across every AREC service, so one account covers the TMS and every tool we run. It takes about a minute.

    Create an account Already have one
  3. 03

    Wait for the meeting link

    Once we have your account, we email a booking link to the address you used in step 1. Pick a slot that works. In that meeting we work out what you will be doing and when you are needed.

Please use the same email address for both steps. We send the meeting link to the inbox you wrote to first. If it never arrives, reply to your original message and we will send it again.

Roles

Event and logistics is our biggest need and the easiest way in. Most roles are trained on the day.

event and logistics

  • Setup and teardown Build the field, set the barriers and game pieces, then strike it all down at the end of the day.
  • Field marshal Own the field between matches. Reset the game, clear debris, and keep the pace up.
  • Tech inspector Check every robot before it is allowed on the field. Size, weight, hardware, build rules.
  • Safety marshal Run the safety briefing and keep the pit and field areas safe.
  • Queue marshal Call and order the teams, run the ready queue, and start matches on time.
  • Check-in desk Welcome teams, hand out badges and schedules, and answer questions on arrival.

match day

  • Referee Call the match. Know the rules inside out, make the call in the moment, then explain it to the teams.
  • Scorekeeper Track the score and the penalties for every match, and get it entered correctly first time.

workshops and mentoring

  • Build mentor Work with one team through their season, from design to competition day.
  • Workshop facilitator Run hands-on build sessions for new teams. You bring the expertise, we bring the parts and the room.

outreach and media

  • Streamer and announcer Run the event stream and call the matches.
  • Photographer Capture the photos and footage people actually want to see.
  • School outreach Talk to schools, youth groups, and community organizations about putting a team together.
  • Fundraising Find the sponsorships and donations that subsidize kits and keep registration affordable.

What to expect

No experience needed. Almost every event volunteer was new on their first day.

training On the day, before the doors open.
minimum age Depends on the role and the venue.
commitment Often just one event day.
travel Reimbursed where we can.
food Provided on event days.

Who you would be working with

Students, mostly.

AREC has no separate adult organization behind it. Current high school students manage day-to-day operations, tournament logistics, and peer mentorship. Working with us means working directly with the people running the league, and saying so when something is wrong.

If you are not sure where you fit, say so in your email. We will match you to whatever is useful at the time, and you can change roles later.

Say hello.

One email, one account, one short meeting. After that you are part of the team that runs the league.
